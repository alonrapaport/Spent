# Libi Simcha birth announcement

A self-contained, single-page digital invitation (Hebrew, right to left) celebrating the birth of Libi Simcha, daughter of Yael and Alon Rapaport. Built to be shared on WhatsApp and read comfortably on a phone.

- `index.html` - the entire invitation (markup + embedded CSS, Google Fonts, inline SVG art, a small inline script for the countdown and share button). No build step required: open it directly in a browser.
- `libi.jpg` - the featured photo.
- `libi.ics` - calendar event used by the "add to calendar" button.

The event details (Friday, 26.06.26, 12:00, Rishi 19 Apt 1, Beit Shemesh) include one-tap Waze and Google Maps links, an "add to calendar" button, a share button (Web Share with copy-link fallback), a live countdown to the date, and a gentle one-time hearts animation on load (disabled under reduced-motion).
